/09 · EV Charging · Video Understanding

Vandalism Detection — Hybrid CNN-RNN-Attention

6 categories Sliding-window classification across webcam and RTSP streams

Designed and implemented a surveillance-video classifier for EV charging stations using a hybrid EfficientNetB0, Bidirectional LSTM, and Multi-Head Self-Attention architecture. The model recognizes six vandalism activity categories, including car breaking, glass tampering, kiosk-screen damage, cable tampering, and break-ins, while working within the constraints of a very small training dataset.

  • Spatio-temporal architectureUsed EfficientNetB0 for per-frame visual features, a BiLSTM for motion context, and self-attention to focus on the most informative moments in each clip.
  • Small-data strategyOptimized learning from approximately 23 videos through ImageNet transfer learning, multi-clip temporal sampling, Mixup, label smoothing, class-weighted loss, and heavy spatio-temporal augmentation.
  • Progressive fine-tuningTrained in two phases—frozen backbone followed by end-to-end tuning—with warmup and cosine-annealing schedules to reduce overfitting.
  • Production inferenceBuilt multi-clip ensemble prediction, batch evaluation, sliding-window live detection, and confidence-based alerting behind a FastAPI service.
FLOW · Webcam / RTSP → temporal clips → CNN features → BiLSTM + attention → confidence threshold → alert
PyTorch EfficientNetB0 BiLSTM Multi-Head Attention FastAPI OpenCV RTSP
